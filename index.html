Start with one app

Add a public App Store app or a pre-launch app. Choose a storefront and add searches you want to follow. Pull to refresh your workspace. Search positions reflect the results returned by Apple; unavailable results are labeled.

Apple’s store reports

In Settings, connect your own App Store Connect API key. Rankboard requests reports directly from Apple and saves the results on your phone. A new analytics connection may take a day or two to produce reports. These reports are delayed analytics, not live visitors.

Apple Ads is optional and supplies Apple’s popularity scores. Other demand and download figures are estimates, labeled in the app.

Live sessions need an integration

Rankboard cannot see inside an app just because you track its store listing. The app’s developer must add the Rankboard Live Swift package, configure its own CloudKit container and connect that container in Rankboard’s Live setup screen.

Live analytics cover sessions on devices signed into iCloud. The dashboard refreshes roughly once a minute and treats a session as inactive after two minutes without a heartbeat. It shows sessions and named events, not players’ names or a count of unique people.

Download the Swift integration and read the included README for schema, lifecycle hooks, opt-out and retention setup. Use your own entitled container; only apps on Rankboard’s developer team can use Rankboard’s container.

Your copy, on your phone

Your workspace uses local storage and private iCloud sync when available. Settings → Export to Files saves a JSON copy to On My iPhone or iCloud Drive. Private iCloud sync and a file in iCloud Drive are separate copies. Exported files contain analytics, not connection credentials or decryption keys.

Rankboard Pro

Free includes one app and ten keywords. Pro includes five apps and one hundred keywords per app, plus the full activity feed. Prices and billing periods appear in the purchase screen using your App Store region. Restore purchases there if you change devices. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Delete your workspace

Settings → Data & iCloud lets you remove saved workspace data and send those deletions through private iCloud sync. Delete exported files separately in Files. This does not delete live records held in a tracked app’s CloudKit container; that app’s developer controls its records and retention.
