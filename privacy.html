Rankboard support

Privacy policy

Rankboard · Updated October 6, 2026

Rankboard stores your workspace on your phone and uses Apple services for optional sync, purchases, store reports and live session integration. It has no separate Rankboard account, advertising network or Rankboard analytics server. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising tracking.

Your workspace and private iCloud

Tracked apps, keywords, rank history, reviews, listing changes, imported store reports and saved live summaries are stored locally. When iCloud is available, this workspace syncs through your private CloudKit database. It is not published as a public workspace. Rankboard’s developer cannot browse your private CloudKit records through the developer console.

A JSON export is created only when you choose Export to Files and a destination. It may contain business-sensitive analytics. Share or store it as you would any private document. Credentials, purchase receipts and live decryption keys are excluded.

Connection credentials

App Store Connect and Apple Ads keys and tokens are saved in Keychain. Your signing private keys stay on the device; Rankboard uses them to sign requests to Apple. Live connection API tokens and recipient decryption keys are also saved in Keychain. Live recipient keys use synchronizable Keychain when iCloud Keychain is enabled. They are not included in workspace exports.

Optional App Store Connect actions use the permissions of the key you supply. You choose whether to prepare a listing change or a product-page draft. Rankboard does not automatically publish a tracked app.

Live session records

Live analytics require the Rankboard Live integration in an app controlled by its developer. That integration writes encrypted records to the tracked app’s CloudKit container. Rankboard reads them from Apple and decrypts them on your phone. The payload contains a fresh foreground-session ID, session times and bounded screen, mode, version and setup-step labels. Developers must not put personal content in those labels.

CloudKit public records expose routing timestamps, encrypted payloads and Apple’s pseudonymous creator-account metadata. Encryption protects session payloads, not that public metadata. The dashboard does not display account identities or names, and it does not claim to count unique people. Coverage is limited to devices signed into iCloud.

Each tracked app’s developer controls its container, privacy disclosure, player opt-out and retention. The SDK remembers this installation’s session records locally, attempts to delete them on opt-out, and attempts to remove records older than seven days when that installation next starts a session. An offline, uninstalled or inactive installation cannot perform that cleanup. The developer must maintain container-wide retention and handle deletion failures. Inactive display status is not physical deletion. Deleting Rankboard’s workspace does not delete those separate records.

Purchases and optional permissions

Apple handles subscription payments. Rankboard verifies purchase status on your device with StoreKit and does not send receipts to a Rankboard server. Optional notifications announce available rank/review changes or a morning brief. Calendar write access is requested only when you choose to add a task to your calendar.

Network use

Rankboard retrieves public App Store results and images and, when you connect them, reports and scores from Apple’s APIs. Apple processes those requests under its own policies. This public support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard network information such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The website has no analytics scripts, forms or advertising cookies.

Deletion and support

Use Settings → Data & iCloud to delete workspace data and sync those deletions. Remove exported files separately in Files, and disconnect integrations to remove their saved credentials. Apple manages your iCloud account and subscription. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Please do not send credentials.

Changes

This page will be updated when Rankboard’s data handling changes.
